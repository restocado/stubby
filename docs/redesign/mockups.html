Stubby redesign: high-fidelity mockups

The same six screens as the wireframes, now with a real look: colour, type, icons, status chips, and the invented test receipts as the pictures. Desktop leads, because that is where receipts are checked. The phone versions follow, then the small parts and a style guide.

Choices that were not yours to make yet, so they are easy to change: the look (a calm, paper-and-ink money app with a deep green accent, drawn from scratch and not Bootstrap) and the home page name ("Inbox"). There are no thumbnails (decided): a row is recognised by merchant, date and amount, and a PDF carries a small PDF tag. The big receipt picture on the Review screen stays, because that is where it is checked. The numbers, including the 12-month chart and the comparison with last month, are made up for the picture. The receipts shown are the invented ones in testset/.
