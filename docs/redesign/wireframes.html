Stubby redesign: low-fidelity wireframes

Grey boxes only. The point is where things go and what each screen is for, not how it looks. Desktop leads. It is where receipts are checked and approved, so the desktop layouts come first and carry the detail. The phone layouts show how each screen collapses, and they still have to work, mostly for adding a receipt on the go. The screenshots of today's app are in screenshots/, and the reasoning is in README.md.

  • Approve & next is the primary action: a pile of receipts is a loop, and today every approval drops you back at the list. It goes oldest first, the order the receipts were added and read, and "1 of 3" says where you are. Plain Approve still exists, and so does Skip, which moves a receipt to the end of the round without changing it.
  • On a desktop the loop can run from the keyboard: Enter approves and moves on, the arrow keys go back and forward, S skips, Esc returns to the inbox. The shortcuts are shown on the page, and are off while a field has focus so typing a correction never approves anything.
  • The receipt gets real room and zoom, rotate and fit controls, because blurry and tilted photos need to be inspected. Today the picture is small and cannot be zoomed.
  • The total is large and right-aligned, with a thousands separator. The category is a row of chips, so it is one tap and not a dropdown. The date shows a month name and not 09/14/2026.
  • Why it was flagged is a short line above the form. What the model read stays one click away, so a correction is visible.
  • Delete is not on the page: it is in the menu, with a confirmation. On a phone Approve sticks to the bottom edge.
